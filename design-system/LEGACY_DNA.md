# Gorilla HIS Legacy Product DNA — Evolution Standard v1.0

## Purpose

Gorilla HIS mockups must evolve the existing product, not redesign it from zero.

The current production/real HIS is the continuity baseline. New mockups may improve hierarchy, spacing, typography, surface craft, interaction and information architecture, but must still be recognizable as the same Gorilla HIS product family.

Core principle:

`Gorilla HIS Evolution > Clean-sheet Redesign`

## Mandatory Legacy DNA Scan

Before Visual Concept and before coding, review the relevant real Gorilla HIS screenshots in `screenshots/actual-gorilla-his/`.

For the target module/archetype, produce a concise table:

| Legacy element | PRESERVE | IMPROVE | REPLACE | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|

At minimum inspect when relevant:
- application shell / top bar / module navigation;
- patient header / patient context;
- tabs and section navigation;
- worklist / table density;
- form/control language;
- primary/secondary action placement;
- status/alert treatment;
- typography scale and information density;
- spacing rhythm;
- clinical context placement.

Do not copy obvious weaknesses blindly. Do not discard familiar Gorilla behavior merely because a cleaner generic pattern exists.

## Continuity Rule

A new screen must preserve enough recurring product grammar that an existing Gorilla HIS user can recognize the product without relying only on the logo or brand color.

If the new mockup feels like a different vendor/product family, Legacy Continuity FAILS even if the page is individually attractive.

## Evolution Rule

Allowed improvement areas:
- stronger information hierarchy;
- clearer patient/task context;
- more disciplined spacing and alignment;
- better typography hierarchy;
- better control states and interaction feedback;
- better use of surface depth;
- clearer decision path;
- reduction of visual noise;
- more deliberate operational/clinical visualization.

Do not modernize by default through:
- changing the entire shell language;
- replacing familiar HIS density with consumer-app whitespace;
- replacing established navigation with an unrelated fashionable layout;
- converting every section into cards;
- introducing a new icon/theme language;
- making a clinical workspace look like an LMS, CRM, fintech or AI dashboard.

## BMW Evolution Test

BMW-level craft means the new Gorilla HIS should feel like a more refined generation of the same product, not like a completely unrelated vehicle.

Ask:
1. What recognizable Gorilla HIS DNA is preserved?
2. What legacy friction is materially improved?
3. What crafted detail makes the new generation feel better to operate?
4. Would an existing Gorilla user still know where they are and how to work?

If answers to 1 or 4 are weak, do not call the concept successful.

## Evidence Rule

Design Notes must contain:
- screenshots reviewed;
- Preserve / Improve / Replace decisions;
- continuity risks;
- deliberate deviations and rationale.

If no relevant screenshot exists, declare:
`LEGACY REFERENCE LIMITATION — no directly relevant real Gorilla HIS screenshot available.`

Do not invent legacy behavior.
